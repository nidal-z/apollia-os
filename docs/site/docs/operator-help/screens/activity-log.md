# The Activity log screen

> Every action of the agents and every change of access, in plain words.

## What it is for

The Activity log lists what the agents did and what changed in their accesses, most recent first. It stays on this device.

## What you can do

- Filter the entries to find an action or a change.
- Open an entry to read its detail in the detail panel.
- Export the entries that match the filters in force.
