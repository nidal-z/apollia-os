# The Developer screen

> The entry of those who plug their own programs into Apollia.

## What it is for

The Developer screen is meant for people who connect their own programs to Apollia. It is listed in the sidebar only while the developer mode is on. Day to day, nothing here is needed to work with agents.

## What you can do

- Turn the developer mode on or off in Settings, section Developer.
- Reach the technical screens the mode adds to the sidebar: Transcriptions, Observability and Memory.
