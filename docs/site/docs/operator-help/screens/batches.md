# The history of the batches

> Every batch, most recent first, whatever the agent.

## What it is for

A batch is one task handed to an agent for a series of items: the rows of an Excel or CSV file, the files of a folder, or the messages of a mailbox. This screen, reached with **Batch history** at the top of the Agents screen, keeps every batch that was run, with its agent, its source, its outcome and how many of its items were processed.

## What you can do

- Search a batch by its file or its agent, and filter the list by agent or by outcome.
- Open a batch to read it again: its view shows what you asked, the files it produced and its results.
- Export what a batch produced.
- Run the failures of a batch again.
